Explore the Main Islands

Each Hawaiian island offers a totally different vibe and landscape. Click through your favorite attractions and guides to plan your dream island hopping itinerary!

Oʻahu: The vibrant heart of Hawaii, home to Honolulu, historic landmarks, world-class food trucks, and legendary surf beaches.

Maui: Known as the Valley Isle, famous for the winding Road to Hana, towering Haleakalā sunrise, and stunning coastlines.

Hawaiʻi Island (The Big Island): A massive island featuring active volcanoes, sacred peaks like Maunakea, and nighttime manta ray snorkeling.

Kauaʻi: The Garden Isle, famous for the jaw-dropping, emerald-green cliffs of the Nā Pali Coast and secluded wilderness beaches.

Discover the Hawaiian Islands

Hawaii is a group of islands in the Pacific Ocean known for its beautiful beaches, green mountains, volcanoes, and tropical scenery. Each island has its own unique landscapes and activities for visitors to enjoy.

Whether you want to explore nature, see amazing views, learn about Hawaiian culture, or enjoy the ocean, Hawaii has many exciting experiences to offer!

What Makes Hawaii Special

  • Beautiful beaches and clear blue water
  • Volcanoes, mountains, and rainforests
  • Unique plants and wildlife
  • Native Hawaiian history and culture

Quick Island Fun Fact

Did you know that the Hawaiian archipelago is actually made up of over 130 islands, islets, and atolls stretched across 1,500 miles of the Pacific Ocean? However, only 8 main islands make up the state of Hawaii, and 6 of them are open to visitors!
